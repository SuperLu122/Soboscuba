soboscuba

Pull back. Let go. Merge the sobos.

  • Drag back from anywhere and let go to fling the sobo. Pull further for a harder shot.
  • Tap to drop it gently straight down.
  • Equal numbers merge: 1 + 1 = 2, 2 + 2 = 4, all the way up to 16k.
  • Don't squash the pressure pads, the domes on the side walls. Press one too hard and its bar on the lid fills up. A full bar ends the game.
